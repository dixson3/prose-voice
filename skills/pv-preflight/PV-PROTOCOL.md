# Prose Voice Triggers

When writing or reviewing persuasive or explanatory narrative essays, load `pv-prosevoice`.
Also invoke it after editing an identified essay and before presenting essay prose you authored
or rewrote this turn. Follow its preflight, exclusions and write-authority rules; preflight
handles missing guidance. Unrelated Markdown, intermediate artifacts and exports do not trigger
it. The skill owns the workflow; this rule only reminds the agent when to load it.
